# Warehouse Safety Rules

Owner: Health, Safety & Environment · Version: 1.4 · Effective: 1 January 2026

## Who this applies to

Everyone who enters a Brightwater warehouse floor: employees, agency staff, drivers, contractors and visitors. Nobody may work on the floor without completing the half-day **safety induction**. Visitors must be escorted at all times.

## Personal protective equipment (PPE)

- **High-visibility vest** (yellow for staff, orange for visitors and drivers) at all times.
- **S3 safety shoes** with toe caps.
- Cut-resistant gloves when handling strapping or stretch-wrap.
- Hearing protection in areas marked with the blue ear sign (sorter and compactor zones).

## Traffic rules

- Speed limit for forklifts and reach trucks: **10 km/h** inside, **15 km/h** in the outside yard.
- Pedestrians use the **green walkways** only. Forklifts give way at marked crossings.
- No phones, earphones or eating while operating equipment.
- Only staff with a valid internal **forklift licence** (renewed every **3 years**) may drive. The licence card must be carried.
- Pre-use equipment check every shift, recorded on the scanner.

## Manual handling

- Maximum manual lift for one person: **23 kg**. Heavier items need two people or a lifting aid.
- Bend your knees, keep the load close, do not twist.
- Pallets may not be stacked above **1.8 m** on the floor (block stacking).

## Racking

- Never climb racking.
- Racking is inspected **weekly** by trained staff and **yearly** by an external expert.
- Report any damaged upright or beam immediately; the bay is closed off with red tape until repaired.

## Reporting

- Report every accident, injury or near miss to your shift leader and log it in the **SafeLog** app within **24 hours**.
- Serious injuries are SEV1 incidents: call 112, then the Site Safety Officer and the Duty Manager (+31 10 000 0911).

## Fire and evacuation

- Keep fire exits and extinguishers clear (1 m free zone).
- On the alarm, stop equipment, leave by the nearest exit and go to the **assembly point** (Rotterdam: car park P2; Hamburg: gate 3; Lyon: front lawn).
- Fire drills take place twice a year at every site.
