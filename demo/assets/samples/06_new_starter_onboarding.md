# New Starter Onboarding

Owner: People & Culture · Version: 1.2 · Effective: 1 April 2026

## Before day one

- Your manager assigns you an **onboarding buddy** from your team at least 5 working days before you start.
- You receive a welcome email with your start time (09:00 in offices, 07:00 for warehouse shifts), the address and a parking code.
- Bring a valid ID and your signed contract.

## First-week checklist

### Day 1 (Monday)
- [ ] Collect your access badge and visitor-free entry at reception.
- [ ] Receive your laptop and phone from IT; set up your password (14+ characters) and MFA.
- [ ] Meet your manager and buddy; agree goals for your first 30 days.
- [ ] Office or site tour, including fire exits and the assembly point.

### Day 2
- [ ] Complete the **IT Security Basics** e-learning (45 minutes).
- [ ] Log in to the Harbor HR portal and check your personal and bank details.
- [ ] Set up BW Connect VPN and test it.

### Day 3
- [ ] Warehouse and operations staff: **safety induction** (half day) before entering any warehouse floor. This is mandatory and nobody may work on the floor without it.
- [ ] Office staff: shadow a colleague in Customer Service for two hours.

### Day 4
- [ ] Read the Code of Conduct and confirm you have read it in Harbor.
- [ ] Join your team's regular meetings and chat channels.

### Day 5 (Friday)
- [ ] First-week check-in with your manager (30 minutes).
- [ ] Give feedback on your onboarding via the short survey in Harbor.

## First 30 days

- All mandatory e-learning (security, code of conduct, data protection) must be finished **within 14 calendar days** of your start date.
- Your probation period is **2 months**. A formal probation review takes place in week 7.
- New starters work fully on site for the first 4 weeks (see the Remote Work Policy).
