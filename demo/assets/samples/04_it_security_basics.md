# IT Security Basics

Owner: IT & Information Security · Version: 1.5 · Effective: 15 January 2026

## Why this matters

Brightwater handles customer shipment data, customs documents and payment details. One compromised account can stop a warehouse. These rules apply to every employee, contractor and temporary worker.

## Passwords

- Minimum length: **14 characters**. Use a passphrase of four or more random words.
- Never reuse your Brightwater password on any other site.
- Passwords do **not** expire on a schedule; change yours immediately if you think it is compromised.
- Store passwords only in the company password manager, **VaultBox**. Never in spreadsheets, notes or browsers.
- After **10 failed attempts** your account locks for 30 minutes.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

- MFA is mandatory for email, the HR portal, Ledgerly, the VPN and all customer systems.
- Use the company authenticator app on your work or personal phone. SMS codes are allowed only as a temporary fallback for 7 days.
- Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start. Report unexpected prompts as a security incident.

## Devices

- Screens lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity; lock manually (Windows + L) when you walk away.
- USB storage devices are blocked. Use the company file share for transfers.
- Install software only from the Brightwater Software Center.

## If your laptop or phone is lost or stolen

1. Call the IT Service Desk at **+31 10 000 0100** (24/7) **within 1 hour** of noticing.
2. IT will lock and remotely wipe the device.
3. Report theft to the local police and upload the police report reference to the IT ticket within 48 hours.
4. Your manager approves a replacement device, normally issued within 2 working days.

## Phishing

- Forward suspicious emails as an attachment to **phishing@brightwater.example.com**, or use the *Report Phishing* button in Outlook.
- Do not click links, open attachments or reply.
- If you already clicked or entered your password, call the IT Service Desk immediately and change your password.
- Quarterly phishing simulations are run; employees who fail twice complete a 20-minute refresher course.

## Mandatory training

Security awareness e-learning must be completed every year by **30 April**.
