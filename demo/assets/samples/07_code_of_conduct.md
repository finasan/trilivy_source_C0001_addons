# Code of Conduct

Owner: Legal & Compliance · Version: 1.0 · Effective: 1 January 2026

## Our commitment

Brightwater Logistics Ltd moves goods for thousands of customers across Europe. They trust us with their products, their data and their reputation. This Code applies to every employee, director, contractor and agency worker, in every location.

## Respect at work

- We treat colleagues, drivers, customers and suppliers with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.
- We use English as the common language in mixed meetings, so no one is excluded.
- Managers must address reported concerns within **5 working days**.

## Gifts and hospitality

- You may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to **€75** per person per occasion.
- Anything above €75 must be declined or registered with Compliance in the Gift Register within **5 working days**.
- Cash, vouchers and gifts during an active tender are never allowed, whatever the value.

## Conflicts of interest

- Declare any personal interest in a supplier, customer or competitor (including family members) to Compliance.
- Every employee completes the annual conflict-of-interest declaration in Harbor by **31 January**.

## Anti-bribery and fair competition

- We never offer or accept bribes or facilitation payments, including to customs or border officials.
- We do not discuss prices, tenders or capacity with competitors.

## Data and confidentiality

- Customer shipment data is confidential. Share it only with people who need it for their work.
- Follow the IT Security Basics rules for passwords, devices and phishing.

## Speaking up

If you see something that breaks this Code:

1. Talk to your manager, or if that is not possible,
2. Contact Compliance at ethics@brightwater.example.com, or
3. Use the confidential **Speak Up line** on **+31 10 000 0199** or the anonymous web form. Both are available 24/7 in English, Dutch, German and French.

Reports are acknowledged within **7 days**. Brightwater does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Consequences

Breaches can lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal, and may be reported to the authorities.
