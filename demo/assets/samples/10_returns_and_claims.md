# Returns and Claims

Owner: Customer Service · Version: 1.2 · Effective: 1 March 2026

## Inspect at delivery

The receiver should check every shipment before signing the delivery note (CMR or POD).
- **Visible damage or missing items:** write a clear note on the CMR/POD at delivery ("2 cartons crushed", "1 pallet missing") and take photos. Signing "clean" makes a later claim much harder.

## Claim deadlines

| Situation | Deadline to notify Brightwater |
|---|---|
| Visible damage or shortage | At delivery, noted on CMR/POD, confirmed in writing within **2 working days** |
| Concealed damage (found after unpacking) | Within **7 calendar days** of delivery |
| Total loss / non-delivery | Within **21 days** after the expected delivery date |

The complete claim file must reach us within **30 days** of delivery. Late claims are declined.

## Documents needed

1. Completed **Claim Form BW-CL1** (download from the customer portal).
2. Copy of the CMR or POD with the damage remark.
3. Photos of the damage, the packaging and the pallet label.
4. Commercial invoice showing the value of the goods.
5. Repair estimate or proof of write-off.
6. Tracking number (starts with **BWL**).

Send the file to claims@brightwater.example.com or upload it in the portal under *Claims*.

## Handling and compensation

- Claims are acknowledged within **2 working days** and decided within **30 days** of receiving a complete file.
- Standard liability follows the CMR Convention: max **8.33 SDR per kg** gross weight of the damaged goods.
- Customers with Cargo Plus insurance (included in the Enterprise tier, optional for others) are covered up to the full invoice value, up to **€100,000** per shipment.
- Do not dispose of damaged goods until the claim is closed; we may inspect them.

## Returns (reverse logistics)

- Request a **Return Authorisation (RA) number** in the portal before sending any goods back.
- Returns must be collected within **14 days** of the RA being issued.
- Return collections are charged at the standard rate for the zone, unless the return is caused by a Brightwater error.
