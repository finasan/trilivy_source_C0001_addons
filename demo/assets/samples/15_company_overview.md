# Company Overview

Owner: Corporate Communications · Version: 1.2 · Effective: 1 January 2026

*Brightwater Logistics Ltd is a fictional company created for product demonstrations.*

## Who we are

Brightwater Logistics Ltd is a mid-size European freight and warehousing company. We store, pick, pack and deliver goods for around **1,200 business customers** in retail, food ingredients, automotive parts and e-commerce.

## History

- **1987:** Founded in Rotterdam as *Brightwater Haulage*, with 6 trucks serving the port.
- **1998:** Opened the first cross-dock warehouse at Rotterdam Waalhaven.
- **2004:** Opened the **Hamburg** depot to serve Germany and Scandinavia.
- **2011:** Renamed Brightwater Logistics Ltd; launched contract warehousing.
- **2013:** Opened the **Lyon** hub for France and southern Europe.
- **2022:** Launched the customer portal and tracking API.
- **2025:** Reached 50% electric or HVO-fuelled trucks on urban routes.

## Locations

| Site | Role | Staff | Warehouse space |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rotterdam** (HQ) | Head office, main hub, port operations | ~480 | 95,000 m² |
| **Hamburg** | Germany & Nordics hub, ADR goods | ~260 | 55,000 m² |
| **Lyon** | France & southern Europe hub | ~160 | 35,000 m² |

Total: about **900 employees**, **185,000 m²** of warehousing and a fleet of **210 trucks**. Revenue in 2025 was €214 million.

## Leadership

The company is led by an Executive Committee of six roles:
- Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- Chief Operating Officer (COO), responsible for all three sites
- Chief Information Officer (CIO), responsible for IT and information security
- Chief People Officer (CPO), leading People & Culture
- Chief Commercial Officer (CCO), leading Sales and Customer Service

Each site is run day to day by a **Site Operations Manager** who reports to the COO.

## Our values

1. **Reliable** – we do what we promise, on time (target: 97% on-time delivery).
2. **Safe** – nobody gets hurt at work; safety comes before speed.
3. **Open** – we share information and speak up when something is wrong.
4. **Sustainable** – net-zero urban deliveries by 2030.
