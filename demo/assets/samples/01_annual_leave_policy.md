# Annual Leave Policy

Owner: People & Culture · Version: 1.3 · Effective: 1 January 2026

## Purpose

This policy explains how much paid annual leave Brightwater Logistics Ltd employees receive, how to request it, and what happens to unused days. It applies to all permanent and fixed-term staff in Rotterdam, Hamburg and Lyon. Where local law gives a higher minimum, local law applies.

## Entitlement by seniority

Leave is based on full-time employment (40 hours per week) and is pro-rated for part-time staff.

| Years of service | Annual leave days |
|---|---|
| 0–4 years | 25 days |
| 5–9 years | 27 days |
| 10 years or more | 30 days |

- The extra days start on 1 January of the year after your service anniversary.
- Public holidays are **not** deducted from annual leave (see the Public Holidays 2026 document).
- New starters receive a pro-rated entitlement for their first calendar year, rounded up to the nearest half day.

## Carry-over rule

- You may carry over a **maximum of 5 unused days** into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days **must be used by 31 March**. Any carried-over days not used by then expire.
- Exceptions (for example long-term sick leave or parental leave) must be approved in writing by the People & Culture Business Partner.
- Leave is not paid out in cash, except on leaving the company.

## How to request leave

1. Log in to the **Harbor** HR portal and open *Time Off → New Request*.
2. Select the dates and the leave type (Annual, Carry-over, Unpaid).
3. Submit the request:
   - at least **10 working days in advance** for absences longer than 3 consecutive days;
   - at least **2 working days in advance** for shorter absences.
4. Your line manager approves or declines within **3 working days**. If no decision is made, the request escalates automatically to the department head.

## Peak periods

Warehouse and operations teams have a leave freeze from **15 November to 5 January** (peak season). Exceptions need approval from the Site Operations Manager.
