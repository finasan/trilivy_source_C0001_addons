# Pricing Tiers 2026

Owner: Sales & Commercial · Version: 1.1 · Effective: 1 January 2026

## Overview

Brightwater offers three warehousing and distribution tiers. All prices are in EUR, excluding VAT, and apply to contracts signed or renewed in 2026. Freight charges per shipment are quoted separately per zone.

## Onboarding fees

- Essential: €750 one-time
- Professional: €2,000 one-time (includes API integration support, 10 hours)
- Enterprise: quoted per project

## Discounts

- **5% discount** on the monthly platform fee for annual prepayment.
- Multi-site contracts (two or more of Rotterdam, Hamburg, Lyon): an additional 3%.
- Discounts cannot be combined with promotional offers.

## Payment terms

- Invoices are issued monthly in arrears and are payable within **30 days**.
- Late payments carry interest of 1% per month.
- Prices are indexed once a year on **1 January** in line with the Dutch CPI, capped at 4%.

## Changing tiers

Upgrades take effect from the first day of the next month. Downgrades are possible at contract renewal with **60 days' notice**.
