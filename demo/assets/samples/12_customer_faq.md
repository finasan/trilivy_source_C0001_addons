# Customer FAQ

Owner: Customer Service · Version: 1.3 · Effective: 1 June 2026

## 1. How do I book a shipment?
Book through the customer portal or the booking API. You receive a tracking number starting with **BWL** immediately after booking.

## 2. What is the latest time I can book for collection today?
It depends on the destination zone: 17:00 for Benelux (Zone A), 16:00 for Germany and France (Zone B), 14:00 for other EU countries (Zone C) and 12:00 for the UK, Switzerland and Norway (Zone D).

## 3. How long does delivery to Germany take?
Germany is Zone B: standard delivery in 1–2 working days. Next-Day Priority is available when booked by 14:00 for a 35% surcharge.

## 4. Do you deliver on Saturdays?
Saturday delivery is available in Zone A only, on request, with a surcharge of €60 per shipment.

## 5. How do I track my shipment?
Enter your BWL tracking number in the portal. Professional and Enterprise customers can also use the tracking API. Proof of delivery is available within 2 hours of delivery.

## 6. My goods arrived damaged. What should I do?
Note the damage on the CMR/POD at delivery and take photos. For concealed damage, tell us within 7 calendar days. Send the full claim file (form BW-CL1, photos, invoice) within 30 days of delivery.

## 7. How do I return goods?
Request a Return Authorisation (RA) number in the portal first. Goods must be collected within 14 days of the RA being issued.

## 8. Do you ship dangerous goods?
Yes, ADR-classified goods are accepted from Hamburg and Rotterdam with prior approval. They add 1 working day to the standard delivery time.

## 9. Which service tier do I need?
Essential suits up to 200 pallet positions; Professional up to 750 and includes API access and an account manager; Enterprise is for larger volumes. See the Pricing Tiers document.

## 10. How can I reach Customer Service?
Call +31 10 000 0300 (Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00 CET) or email service@brightwater.example.com. We reply to emails within 4 business hours. Enterprise customers also have a 24/7 priority line.
