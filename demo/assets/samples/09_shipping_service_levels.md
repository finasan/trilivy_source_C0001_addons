# Shipping Service Levels

Owner: Operations · Version: 1.6 · Effective: 1 January 2026

## Overview

This document defines the standard delivery times (SLAs) Brightwater Logistics Ltd commits to for palletised and parcel freight. Times are in working days (Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays at origin and destination) and start from collection.

## Delivery zones and SLAs

| Zone | Countries | Standard delivery | Order cut-off for same-day collection |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zone A** | Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg | Next working day | **17:00** |
| **Zone B** | Germany, France | 1–2 working days | **16:00** |
| **Zone C** | Other EU countries | 2–4 working days | **14:00** |
| **Zone D** | United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway | 3–5 working days (customs) | **12:00** |

Cut-off times are local time at the collecting depot (Rotterdam, Hamburg or Lyon). Orders received after the cut-off are collected the next working day.

## Express options

- **Same-Day Rotterdam:** delivery within the Rotterdam–The Hague metro area when booked by **11:00**. Surcharge €95 per shipment.
- **Next-Day Priority (Zone B):** guaranteed next working day to Germany and France when booked by 14:00. Surcharge 35% on the standard rate.
- **Timed delivery:** delivery before 10:00 or 12:00 at Zone A and B addresses, surcharge €40.

## Performance targets

- On-time delivery target: **97%** per calendar month, measured per zone.
- Proof of delivery (POD) available in the customer portal within **2 hours** of delivery.
- If monthly on-time performance falls below 95% in a zone, Professional and Enterprise customers receive a service credit of **5%** of that month's freight charges for that zone (see Pricing Tiers).

## Exclusions

SLAs do not apply to:
- dangerous goods (ADR), which add 1 working day,
- addresses on islands or in mountain areas above 1,500 m,
- delays caused by customs inspections, strikes, extreme weather or incorrect address data.

## Track & trace

Every shipment receives a tracking number beginning with **BWL** and can be followed in the customer portal or via the tracking API (Professional and Enterprise tiers).
