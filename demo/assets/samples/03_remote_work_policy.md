# Remote Work Policy

Owner: People & Culture · Version: 1.2 · Effective: 1 March 2026

## Principle

Brightwater Logistics Ltd works hybrid. Office-based roles combine time in the office with work from home. Roles that require physical presence (warehouse operatives, drivers, yard planners, site security and maintenance) are **not eligible** for remote work.

## How much remote work

- Eligible employees may work remotely **up to 2 days per week**.
- **Tuesday** is the company-wide office day; every office-based team is on site that day.
- New starters work fully on site for their **first 4 weeks**.
- Remote days are agreed with your line manager and recorded in the Harbor HR portal under *Work Location*.

## Core hours and availability

- Core hours are **10:00 to 15:00** local time. You must be reachable by chat and phone during core hours.
- Camera on for team meetings unless agreed otherwise.
- Customer-facing teams must keep the shared service phone line staffed from 08:00 to 18:00.

## Working from abroad

- You may work from another EU country for **up to 20 working days per calendar year**.
- A request must be submitted at least **15 working days in advance** and approved by your manager and People & Culture (tax and social-security check).
- Working from outside the EU is not permitted.

## Equipment and allowances

- The company provides a laptop, headset and a second monitor.
- One-time home-office allowance: **€300** (desk chair, desk or lighting), claimed in Ledgerly with receipts within the first 6 months.
- Monthly internet allowance: **€25**, paid automatically with salary for eligible staff.

## Security at home

- Always connect through the **BW Connect** VPN (see the VPN and Wi-Fi Troubleshooting guide).
- Do not print confidential documents at home.
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your laptop, including at home.
- Public Wi-Fi is allowed only with the VPN active.

## Ending remote arrangements

A manager may pause remote work with 2 weeks' notice for business reasons, such as peak season or performance concerns.
