# Public Holidays 2026 – Office Closures

Owner: People & Culture · Version: 1.0 · Effective: 1 January 2026

## How this works

Offices close on the public holidays of their own country. Office staff do not use annual leave for these days. **Warehouses stay open** with a reduced shift on most holidays; warehouse staff who work a public holiday receive a 100% pay supplement or a day off in lieu, taken within 3 months.

Brightwater also closes all offices on **Thursday 24 December** and **Thursday 31 December** from 13:00 (afternoon closure).

## Bridge day

Lyon office staff get **Friday 15 May** off as a company bridge day after Ascension. Rotterdam and Hamburg do not.

## Holidays on weekends

When a holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, no replacement day is given.
