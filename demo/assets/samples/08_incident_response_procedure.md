# Incident Response Procedure

Owner: IT & Information Security · Version: 1.3 · Effective: 1 May 2026

## What is an incident?

Any event that disrupts operations, threatens safety or puts data at risk: a system outage, a cyber attack, a data breach, a warehouse fire, or a major transport disruption.

## Severity levels

| Level | Description | Example | Response time | Updates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SEV1 – Critical** | Site or company-wide stop, confirmed data breach, injury | Warehouse Management System down at a site; ransomware | **15 minutes** | Every 30 min |
| **SEV2 – High** | Major function degraded, many customers affected | Track & trace portal down; one dock unusable | **1 hour** | Every 2 hours |
| **SEV3 – Medium** | Limited impact, workaround exists | Label printers failing in one hall | **4 business hours** | Daily |
| **SEV4 – Low** | Minor issue, single user | One scanner faulty | **Next business day** | On resolution |

## Who to call

- **First contact for any incident:** IT Service Desk, +31 10 000 0100 (24/7).
- **SEV1 and SEV2:** also call the **Duty Manager on-call** at **+31 10 000 0911**.
- **Injury or fire:** call local emergency services (112) first, then the Site Safety Officer.
- **Suspected data breach:** inform the **Data Protection Officer** at dpo@brightwater.example.com.

## Roles

- **Incident Commander** (the Duty Manager for SEV1/SEV2): leads the response and makes decisions.
- **Technical Lead:** investigates and fixes the cause.
- **Communications Lead** (Head of Customer Service): informs customers and staff.
- **Scribe:** keeps the timeline in the incident ticket.

## Timeline for SEV1

1. **0–15 min:** Incident acknowledged, Incident Commander appointed, bridge call opened.
2. **Within 30 min:** First status update to the executive team.
3. **Within 1 hour:** Customer notice published on the status page if customers are affected.
4. **Within 24 hours:** Any personal-data breach reported internally to the DPO, so Brightwater can notify the supervisory authority within the legal **72 hours**.
5. **Within 5 working days** after resolution: blameless post-incident review written and shared.

## After the incident

Every SEV1 and SEV2 needs a post-incident review with root cause, actions, owners and due dates. Actions are tracked monthly by the CIO.
