# Travel and Expenses Policy

Owner: Finance · Version: 1.4 · Effective: 1 February 2026

## Scope

This policy covers business travel and work-related expenses for all Brightwater Logistics Ltd staff. All trips longer than one day must be pre-approved by your line manager in the **Ledgerly** expense tool before booking.

## Per-diem allowances

The per diem covers meals and small incidentals. It is paid per full travel day; half days (departure or return after 14:00) receive 50%.

| Destination | Daily per diem |
|---|---|
| Netherlands | €45 |
| Germany | €50 |
| France | €48 |
| Other EU countries | €55 |
| UK, Switzerland, Norway | €65 |

If a meal is provided (for example at a conference or by a customer), deduct €15 per provided meal.

## Hotels

Book through the company travel portal. Nightly caps, including breakfast and taxes:

- Rotterdam: €140
- Hamburg: €160
- Lyon: €150
- Paris, London, Zurich: €210
- Other cities: €170

Stays above the cap need written approval from the department head.

## Taxis and airports

- Public transport is the default. First class train travel is allowed for journeys over 3 hours.
- Taxis or ride-hailing are allowed **between 22:00 and 06:00**, when carrying heavy equipment, or when no public transport is available within 30 minutes.
- Airport transfers: take the train to Schiphol, Hamburg or Lyon Saint-Exupéry airports. A taxi is allowed only if your flight departs before 07:30 or lands after 22:00.
- Private car use is reimbursed at **€0.23 per km**; parking up to €25 per day.

## Receipts and claims

- Submit all claims with itemised receipts in Ledgerly **within 30 days** of the expense date.
- Claims submitted after **60 days** are rejected unless the Finance Controller approves an exception.
- Lost receipts: complete the Missing Receipt Declaration (max €50 per claim).
- Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary run (25th of each month).

## Not reimbursable

Alcohol (except approved customer entertainment), minibar items, fines, personal upgrades and loyalty-point purchases.
